# Unnamed

## The Flagship for Everyone

> **A premium phone without the usual compromise between polished consumer hardware and genuine ownership.**

**Product:** Unnamed  
**Class:** Mainstream flagship  
**Status:** Product concept / engineering specification

---

# 1. Product Position

Unnamed is the core phone of the `startup-has-no-name` product family.

It is not the cheap model.

It is not the deliberately compromised model.

It is the phone intended for the majority of customers who want a genuinely premium device without needing the extreme hardware envelope of the Pro.

The target is simple:

> **Make the default product so good that customers do not need the Pro unless they specifically want the extra capability.**

---

# 2. Design Goals

Unnamed prioritizes:

- excellent ergonomics;
- flagship performance;
- exceptional battery life;
- excellent cameras;
- premium display;
- reliable thermals;
- long software support;
- repairability;
- modern connectivity;
- transparent software.

It should feel like a finished consumer product, not a developer prototype.

---

# 3. Industrial Design

Target characteristics:

- premium metal or advanced alloy frame;
- durable front and rear materials;
- carefully engineered antenna windows;
- symmetrical construction where practical;
- serviceable internal assemblies;
- minimal unnecessary decorative elements;
- comfortable grip;
- strong structural rigidity.

The design should prioritize durability and usability over arbitrary thinness.

A thinner phone is not automatically a better phone.

---

# 4. Display

### Target

- 6.6–6.8 inch class;
- premium OLED;
- LTPO;
- adaptive 1–120 Hz or better;
- high outdoor brightness;
- HDR;
- high touch sampling;
- accurate color reproduction;
- excellent low-brightness behavior.

### Engineering requirements

The display must be tested for:

- peak brightness;
- sustained brightness;
- PWM/flicker behavior;
- color accuracy;
- touch latency;
- burn-in resistance;
- power efficiency.

---

# 5. Compute

Unnamed should use a current-generation flagship mobile SoC appropriate for the production year.

The selection criteria are:

- CPU performance;
- GPU performance;
- NPU capability;
- modem quality;
- ISP quality;
- efficiency;
- driver support;
- long-term software support;
- thermal behavior.

The company will not select a chip solely because it wins a synthetic benchmark.

---

# 6. Memory

Target:

- 12 GB base;
- 16 GB premium configuration.

Higher configurations may be introduced if testing shows a real user benefit.

The objective is long-term multitasking and software longevity.

---

# 7. Storage

Target:

- 256 GB;
- 512 GB.

Storage should use a current high-performance mobile standard appropriate for the manufacturing generation.

---

# 8. Camera System

The camera system should be small in number and high in quality.

### Main camera

Target:

- large modern sensor;
- optical image stabilization;
- fast autofocus;
- high-quality optics;
- strong HDR;
- high-quality low-light performance.

### Ultrawide

Target:

- high-resolution sensor;
- autofocus preferred;
- macro capability through autofocus rather than a dedicated low-quality macro camera.

### Telephoto

Target:

- approximately 3x optical range;
- optical stabilization preferred;
- high-quality computational zoom beyond native optical range.

### Front camera

Target:

- high-quality sensor;
- autofocus;
- strong HDR;
- high-quality video.

---

# 9. Camera Software

The image pipeline should prioritize:

- natural detail;
- controlled sharpening;
- realistic skin tones;
- consistent color between cameras;
- motion preservation;
- low-light detail;
- stable HDR.

The phone should not produce photographs that look impressive for five seconds and strange forever.

---

# 10. Battery

Target capacity:

**approximately 6,500–8,000 mAh class**, subject to final industrial design, battery chemistry, weight, and certification.

The battery system should prioritize:

- high energy density;
- long cycle life;
- thermal stability;
- battery health controls;
- serviceability.

---

# 11. Charging

Target:

- high-speed wired charging;
- intelligent charging limits;
- thermal monitoring;
- USB-C;
- wireless charging where it can be integrated without compromising thermal or structural requirements.

Charging performance must be measured from:

- 0–50%;
- 0–80%;
- 0–100%;
- sustained charging temperature.

---

# 12. Thermal System

Unnamed should use passive high-performance thermal engineering.

Target components:

- vapor chamber;
- graphite;
- heat spreaders;
- thermally optimized frame regions;
- high-quality thermal interface materials.

The phone should sustain performance without becoming dangerously hot or aggressively throttling after a few minutes.

---

# 13. Connectivity

Target:

- 5G;
- Wi-Fi 7-class connectivity where available;
- Bluetooth;
- NFC;
- modern GNSS;
- USB-C;
- DisplayPort capability where supported by the selected SoC/platform.

---

# 14. Audio and Haptics

Target:

- high-quality stereo speakers;
- strong low-frequency response within physical limits;
- clean voice reproduction;
- high-quality microphones;
- precise vibration motor;
- spatial audio support where appropriate.

Audio should be tuned as part of the hardware system, not added at the end.

---

# 15. Software

Unnamed should ship with a polished operating system based on an open foundation.

The platform should provide:

- clean UI;
- minimal unnecessary applications;
- long-term updates;
- transparent system components;
- strong privacy controls;
- developer documentation.

The phone should be usable without developer knowledge.

---

# 16. Developer Mode

A documented developer workflow should support:

- bootloader unlocking;
- recovery;
- flashing;
- debugging;
- logs;
- supported alternative builds.

Unlocking may require explicit user confirmation and clear security warnings.

---

# 17. Security

Consumer configuration:

- verified boot;
- hardware-backed keys;
- encrypted storage;
- secure biometric authentication;
- protected update path.

Developer configuration:

- controlled unlock;
- clear warning state;
- documented recovery;
- user ownership of the device.

---

# 18. Repairability

Target serviceable assemblies:

- battery;
- display;
- USB/I/O board;
- speaker;
- camera modules;
- buttons;
- haptics;
- selected antenna assemblies.

The exact architecture will be validated against reliability and water-resistance requirements.

---

# 19. Water and Dust Resistance

The target is flagship-level resistance.

However:

> **A rating will only be published after standardized testing.**

The project will not print a water-resistance number on a box because a marketing department wanted one.

---

# 20. Sustainability

Design priorities:

- long support life;
- replaceable high-wear components;
- recycled material where technically appropriate;
- repair documentation;
- responsible packaging;
- component reuse where possible.

The best environmentally friendly phone is often the phone that does not need to be replaced.

---

# 21. Target User

Unnamed is for:

- students;
- professionals;
- creators;
- gamers;
- developers;
- photographers;
- power users;
- ordinary people who simply want a very good phone.

---

# 22. Product Promise

Unnamed should deliver:

**flagship performance + flagship cameras + flagship display + exceptional battery + long ownership + genuine user control.**

The Pro exists for people who want to push those boundaries further.

---

# 23. Engineering Gate

Unnamed is ready for production only when the engineering team can demonstrate:

- sustained performance;
- acceptable thermals;
- reliable battery behavior;
- camera quality targets;
- display targets;
- connectivity stability;
- security requirements;
- repair procedure;
- software update reliability.

---

# 24. Positioning

> **Unnamed is the phone you buy when you want the flagship experience without needing the word "Pro" printed on the box.**
